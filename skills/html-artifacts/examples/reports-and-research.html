Checkout outage: expired payment-gateway certificate

For 47 minutes no card payment went through. The gateway's client certificate expired at 14:02; renewal had been automated, but the renewal job had been failing silently for 30 days. Payments recovered at 14:49 after a manual renewal.

Root cause

The certificate renewal job lost write access to the secrets store during a permissions clean-up in the previous month. It logged an error every night, but its alert was routed to a channel archived in the same clean-up, so nobody saw it. Nothing checked the certificate's expiry date directly.

What worked, what didn't

Worked

  • Error-rate alert fired within 4 minutes.
  • Rollback took 3 minutes and ruled out the deploy cleanly.

Didn't

  • The recent deploy anchored the investigation for 18 minutes.
  • The renewal runbook did not mention the second approver.
